# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A small in-house team: a manager, the person running Google Ads, and the people selling sponsor spots. They check it daily on a laptop or desktop and scan a lot of numbers across many sites at once.

## Product Purpose

Ads engine runs a network of about 200 websites as one business. The team buys Google Ads traffic to the sites and earns from sponsors and AdSense. The product shows which sites earn more than they cost, what needs attention, and lets the team act on it (pause, set caps, approve budget moves, handle sponsor deals). Success: problems are seen and handled the same day, and money moves towards sites that pay it back.

## Positioning

One view of a whole network of small sites, where every site is judged by money in vs money out and compared with similar sites (its peer group), not looked at alone.

## Operating Context

- Ad platform: Google Ads for now. Meta screens show sample data, and a Meta connection is built and switched off until it's wanted. Google and Meta can be viewed together or one at a time. Results from each stay separate, and a person approves every Meta change.
- Revenue: sponsor deals (fixed monthly fee per spot) and AdSense.
- Sites are grouped by site kind (Local, Region, Topic, Person, Institution, Event; the list is open), group, domain type, country, region and launch cohort. Terms are defined in CONTEXT.md.
- Roles: Viewer, Operator, Manager, Admin (first draft).
- The system suggests changes (budget moves, cap changes); a person always approves.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current stage: clickable frontend mockups with made-up data for discussion with the manager. No backend, no real Google Ads or AdSense calls.
- Stack: Vite + React + TypeScript, plain CSS (existing project).
- Undecided: the contribution formula, the rule for qualified visits, how alerts are detected, the backend (possibly Melvin's DBviewer).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Ads engine". Do not use "Voice Network" anywhere in the product.
- No existing logo, colours or fonts; free hand.

## Evidence on Hand

- Manager's brief: five view descriptions (network command center, cohort and geography, site detail, campaign and channel, sponsor and revenue) from the "Voice Network Optimization Brief" document.
- No real data, customers or screenshots. All numbers in the mockups are invented and must be labelled as sample data.

## Product Principles

1. Money in vs money out is the first thing on every screen.
2. Show what needs a person today before showing everything else.
3. Every number can be traced to where it came from.
4. The system suggests; a person approves.
5. Dense but calm: many sites at a glance without noise.
