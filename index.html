Ads engine Log in

Run ads across a whole network of websites from one dashboard

Ads engine is a dashboard for a small team running about 200 websites as one business. It shows which sites earn more than they cost and what needs attention today, and it keeps ad spending inside set limits.

What it does

  • Shows each site's ad spend, visits, earnings and alerts, compared with similar sites.
  • Lets the team pause campaigns, change daily budgets and set a monthly spending cap.
  • Tracks sponsor deals and earnings next to what each site costs.

How it uses Google data

With the permission of the person who signs in with Google, Ads engine uses the Google Ads API to read campaigns, budgets, spending and conversion settings for the Google Ads accounts the team connects. It also makes the changes the team asks for, and applies the spending cap the team sets. It does not sell this data. Full details are in the privacy policy.
