Ads engine

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Ads engine is a dashboard run by the Ads engine team for managing ads across a network of websites. This policy explains how it accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

Google data we access

When someone signs in with Google and gives permission, Ads engine asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords, which allows it to manage Google Ads accounts through the Google Ads API. It can access this data for the accounts that person can reach and the team has connected:

  • Account IDs and whether an account is a test account.
  • Campaigns, budgets and experiments, including their names, status and settings.
  • Spending and performance figures, and conversion tracking settings.

It does not ask for access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

How we use it

  • To show the team how its campaigns are spending and performing.
  • To make changes the team asks for, such as pausing a campaign, changing a daily budget or running a test.
  • To keep spending inside the monthly cap the team sets, which can lower budgets or pause campaigns automatically.

We use this data only to provide these features. We do not use it for advertising to you, and the app does not use it to train AI models.

How we store it

  • The Google sign-in token is saved in a private file on the team's own server. It is never sent to the browser.
  • The server keeps logs of the actions it takes, which can include account IDs, campaign names, budget amounts and spending figures.
  • Other Ads data is read from Google when it's needed and is not kept in a database.

How we share it

We don't sell Google user data or share it with anyone outside the team, unless the law requires it. It is sent only between the team's own server and Google. Ads engine's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website

The public copy of the dashboard shows made-up sample data and is not connected to any Google account. It remembers that you've logged in by saving a small note in your browser. It has no analytics or advertising trackers.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove Ads engine's access at any time in your Google Account permissions. To have the saved token and logs about your accounts deleted, email us at either address below.
